HEALTH, SAFETY & FAQ

Your safety is our utmost priority. We operate in strict compliance with the Texas Department of State Health Services (DSHS).

What are your cleanliness and hygiene standards? ▾
One Love Tattoos operates as a fully licensed and inspected studio with the Texas Department of Health. All tattoo and piercing needles, gloves, ink caps, and razors are 100% single-use and opened directly in front of you. Reusable machine tubes and clamps undergo multi-stage ultrasonic cleaning and medical-grade autoclave sterilization with weekly spore testing.
Do you accept walk-ins or are appointments required? ▾
We welcome walk-ins daily from 12:00 PM to 8:00 PM for smaller pieces, flash designs, and piercings on a first-come, first-served basis! For large-scale custom tattoos, sleeve projects, and specific artist bookings, we recommend scheduling an advance consultation via our booking form.
How do deposits work for tattoo appointments? ▾
To secure an appointment date and begin custom drawing, we require a non-refundable deposit ranging between $50 and $250 (depending on the scale of the design). Your deposit goes directly towards the final cost of your tattoo on the day of your session.
What are the age requirements in Texas? ▾
In the State of Texas, you must be at least 18 years of age with a valid government-issued photo ID (Driver's License, State ID, Passport, Military ID) to receive a tattoo. No exceptions. Minors seeking body piercings must have a parent or legal guardian present with proper documentation.
How should I prepare for my tattoo appointment? ▾
Get a good night's rest, stay hydrated, and eat a solid meal 1 to 2 hours before your appointment. Wear comfortable clothing that provides easy access to the area being tattooed. Avoid heavy alcohol or blood-thinning medications 24 hours prior to your session.
